## 📄 Full Description (4000 char max)

ChronosBooth is the AI-powered time machine for your photos. Transform yourself, your partner, or your entire crew into any era — from Ancient Rome and Medieval kingdoms to the roaring '20s, the swinging '60s, and beyond.

**🌟 Four Powerful Modes**

📸 **Solo Mode** — Transform your single photo into any historical era, cinematic style, or fantasy scenario. See yourself as a Renaissance noble, a 1950s movie star, or a futuristic explorer.

💑 **Partners Mode** — Upload photos of you and your partner and watch both of you get transformed together into the same era. Perfect for couples who want to time-travel side by side.

👥 **Group Mode** — Transform your entire crew at once. Friends, family, teammates — everyone gets placed into the same cinematic era together.

🎨 **LoRA Trainer** — Train a personal AI model on your own photos (5–10 images) for maximum identity consistency across every transformation. Your face, perfectly preserved in every era.

**✨ Features**

• 🎭 Dozens of historical eras and cinematic styles to choose from
• 💑 Automated "Couples Eras" library — curated era packs designed for two
• 🎬 Cinematic looping videos that bring your portrait to life with Ken Burns motion
• 🔍 Before/After comparison slider to see the transformation
• 🌍 Community Spotlight — share your portraits and discover what others created
• ❤️ Like and comment on community posts
• 🔥 Daily streaks and challenges to keep the creativity flowing
• 🎁 Refer friends to earn bonus transformations
• 📺 Rewarded ads for free extra transformations
• 💎 Pro plan: HD downloads, no watermarks, no ads, premium eras, and video creation
• 📱 Share directly to Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, or via native sharing

**💳 Plans**

• **Free** — Daily transformations with watermark, supported by ads
• **Pro Monthly / Pro Yearly** — Unlimited HD transformations, no watermarks, no ads, cinematic videos, premium era packs, and priority generation

**🔒 Privacy First**

Your photos are processed securely and never shared without your consent. You control whether your portraits appear in the Community Spotlight. Delete your account and all associated data anytime from Settings.

Transform your photos. Travel through time. Share the magic.

## 🔑 If Using Google Login

1. After your first AAB upload, go to Play Console → **Setup** → **App integrity**
2. Copy the **SHA-256 fingerprint** under *App signing key certificate*
3. In Base44: **Publish** → **Mobile app** → **More Actions (⋯)** → **Add Google Play SHA**
4. Paste the fingerprint and save
